Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

DocuAI ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App, including information that may be processed by third-party services used for document scanning and advertising.

DocuAI processes scanned documents and imported images locally on your device. The developer does not upload or store your documents on external servers. However, third-party advertising services may process certain technical and advertising-related information.

Information We Handle

The developer of DocuAI does not directly require or collect personal information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • User account information
  • Precise location
  • Your scanned documents or imported images

The App does not require users to create an account.

Third-party services integrated into the App, including Google AdMob, may automatically process certain technical information as described in the Advertising and Third-Party Services sections below.

Camera Access

DocuAI uses your device camera only to scan documents. Camera access occurs after you choose to use the document-scanning feature and grant the necessary permission.

Captured images and scanned documents are processed locally on your device. The developer does not receive, upload, or store these images on external servers.

Gallery and Image Access

When you import an image from your device, DocuAI accesses only the image or images you select through the device's file or photo-selection interface.

Selected images are processed locally and are not uploaded to servers operated by the developer.

Document Processing

Document-processing features may include:

  • Document scanning
  • Image importing
  • Perspective correction
  • Image enhancement
  • PDF generation
  • PDF merging
  • Document saving and management

These operations are performed locally on your device. Your documents remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to share, export, move, or delete them.

File Storage

PDF files and related documents created through DocuAI may be stored on your device. The developer does not have access to these locally stored files.

You can delete your documents using the App's available document-management features or your device's file-management tools.

Document Sharing

When you use the Share feature, the selected document is sent only to the application or service you choose through your device's sharing interface.

The privacy practices of the receiving application or service are governed by that application's own privacy policy. DocuAI does not receive or store the documents you share.

Advertising

DocuAI displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google.

Google AdMob may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • General device information
  • Operating-system and application information
  • Advertisement views, clicks, and interactions
  • Diagnostic, performance, and fraud-prevention information
  • Approximate location inferred from an IP address

This information may be used to display advertisements, measure advertising performance, limit repeated advertisements, detect invalid traffic, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services.

Depending on your location, consent choices, device settings, and applicable law, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Where required by applicable law, the App may request your consent before personalized advertising data is processed. You may be able to manage certain advertising preferences through the consent options shown in the App, your Google account, or your device settings.

Analytics and Advertising Measurement

The developer does not use a separate analytics service to analyze your scanned documents or personal files.

However, Google AdMob may collect advertising measurement, diagnostic, and interaction information to provide and evaluate advertisements. This processing is controlled by Google and is subject to Google's applicable policies.

Third-Party Services

DocuAI may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Used to provide and measure advertisements.
  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner: Used to provide document-scanning functionality through Google services available on supported Android devices.
  • Google Play Services: May support application functionality, security, updates, and integrated Google services.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Retention

The developer does not retain copies of your scanned documents, imported images, or generated PDF files on external servers.

Files created through the App remain on your device until you delete them, uninstall the App, clear the App's data, or remove them using your device's storage tools.

Information collected by third-party services may be retained according to their own data-retention policies.

Data Security

DocuAI is designed to process documents locally, reducing the need to transmit document contents over the internet.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and locally stored files. No electronic system or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

DocuAI is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable law to provide valid consent.

The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Third-party advertising services may apply additional restrictions to advertisements shown to users identified as children or users of unknown age.

Your Choices

Depending on your device, location, and applicable law, you may be able to:

  • Grant or deny camera access
  • Select which images or files the App can access
  • Delete locally stored documents
  • Reset or limit your device's advertising identifier
  • Manage advertising preferences through your Google account
  • Withdraw or modify advertising consent where a consent option is provided
  • Uninstall the App to stop future data processing through the App

International Data Processing

Google and its service providers may process advertising-related information in countries other than the country in which you live. Such processing is subject to Google's privacy practices and applicable data-protection safeguards.
