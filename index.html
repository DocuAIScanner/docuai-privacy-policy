Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

DocuAI ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

DocuAI does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

The App does not require:

  • User accounts
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Location information

Camera Access

DocuAI uses your device camera only to scan documents. Camera access occurs only after you choose to scan a document. Images are processed locally on your device. No images are uploaded to our servers.

Gallery Access

When you import images from your gallery, DocuAI accesses only the images you select. No images are uploaded or shared.

Document Processing

All document processing including:

  • Document scanning
  • Perspective correction
  • Image enhancement
  • PDF generation

is performed locally on your device. Your documents never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to share them.

Sharing

If you use the Share feature, the selected document is shared only with the application you choose. DocuAI does not receive or store shared documents.

Advertising

DocuAI does not display advertisements.

Analytics

DocuAI does not use analytics or tracking technologies.

Third-Party Services

DocuAI uses Google ML Kit Document Scanner to provide document scanning functionality.

For more information about Google's privacy practices:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

Because all documents remain on your device, you are responsible for protecting your device and your files.

Children's Privacy

DocuAI is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
